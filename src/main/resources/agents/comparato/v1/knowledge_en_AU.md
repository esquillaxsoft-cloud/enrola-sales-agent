# About Comparato

## Overview
Comparato is an Australian, privately held, technology-enabled company offering a personalised
private health insurance comparison and switching service for eligible Australians. It gives
Australians access to 25 leading private health insurance funds through a single, free,
advisor-led conversation.

Comparato helps people compare policies, understand what they're covered for, and switch funds
where appropriate, without paying anything for the service and without advice influenced by
commercial incentives.

Founded in 2023. Its service is built on human expertise (advisors) rather than algorithmic
comparison tools. Advisor recommendations are not influenced by referral fee amounts.

## Mission
To make private health insurance work for Australians, not the other way around: free, expert,
advisor-led comparison and switching support, guiding customers to cover that suits their health
needs, life stage and budget.

## Cost and how Comparato is paid
- The service is free and no-obligation. Customers pay no fees.
- Premiums are not marked up: the price is the same as buying directly from the health fund.
- Comparato earns a referral fee from the fund the customer chooses, paid via the ItsMy Group.
  Advisors don't know the fee amount, and it doesn't influence recommendations.

## Contracts
- No contracts or lock-in with Comparato. Policies are governed by the health fund's own terms.
- Customers can cancel a policy under standard private health insurance cooling-off rules
  (typically 30 days from the start of the policy).

## Compliance
- Comparato Pty Ltd is a signatory to the Private Health Insurance Intermediaries (PHII) Code of
  Conduct, and holds certification with the Private Health Insurance Association (PHIA).
- All customer calls are recorded and quality-checked against insurer-specific requirements.
